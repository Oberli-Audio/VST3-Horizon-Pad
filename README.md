# Horizon Pad

<!-- Schnellstart (Deutsch) -->
**Schnellstart:** Den Ordner `Horizon Pad.vst3` aus dem Zip nach `~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/`
kopieren (Windows: `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\`) und in der DAW die Plug-ins neu scannen
lassen. Horizon Pad auf eine MIDI-Spur ziehen: es startet mit dem Preset "Lagerfeuer". Zuerst ATTACK
für den Einsatz, FILTER für die Helligkeit und REVERB für den Raum drehen.

A warm, four-layer pad instrument for worship and ambient playing, made by Oberli Audio.
It is a pad and lead synthesiser for a keyboard, not a sample library and not an effect.

## Requirements
Ableton Live 10.1 or newer, also under Rosetta, on macOS 10.13 or newer (Apple Silicon and
Intel) and on Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, with a processor from about 2009 on
(SSE4.2). VST3 only. One instance at a 64-sample buffer uses about a tenth of the time an
Apple M3 Pro core has per block, also under Rosetta; set the buffer to 64 samples or more.

## Install
Latest version: https://github.com/Oberli-Audio/VST3-Horizon-Pad/releases/latest. Download the
zip for your system, not "Source code". Quit your DAW first.

**Windows:** unzip and copy the **whole folder** `Horizon Pad.vst3` (whatever folder it sits
in) to `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\`; Windows asks once for permission. Start your DAW
and rescan the plug-ins (Ableton Live: Preferences > Plug-Ins > Rescan; Ableton Live 10:
Preferences > File/Folder, "Use VST3 Plug-In System Folder" on, Rescan). If the window looks
blurry in Ableton Live, turn off its "Auto-Scale Plug-in Window" for Horizon Pad.

**macOS:** Finder > Go > Go to Folder, paste `~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/`, drop
`Horizon Pad.vst3` there. Start your DAW and let it scan; it says the plugin cannot be verified,
which is expected. **Right after that:** System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down,
**Open Anyway** next to Horizon Pad (the button disappears after a while; if it is gone, scan
again). Then rescan (Ableton Live: Preferences > Plug-Ins > Rescan). Once per download; needs
an administrator account. If the message says "damaged" instead of "cannot be verified", or the button never
appears: Terminal, `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"Horizon Pad.vst3"`,
then rescan.

## Update
Same as install, over the old folder (quit your DAW first). Your presets and settings stay. If the
new version misbehaves, take the previous release from the same page; a project saved with the
newer version opens in the older one unless the release notes say otherwise.

**Coming from a test build made under the old name, Quelle Music:** the DAW now lists Horizon Pad
under Oberli Audio as a new plugin, so projects saved with that build no longer find it. Insert
Horizon Pad again and pick your sound from the preset row: your own presets and the window size
are copied over on the first start. The old `Quelle Music` folders can be deleted after that.

## Uninstall
Delete `Horizon Pad.vst3` from `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\` or
`~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/`. Settings (the window size): `%APPDATA%\Oberli Audio\Horizon Pad\`
or `~/Library/Application Support/Oberli Audio/Horizon Pad/`. Presets:
`Documents\VST3 Presets\Oberli Audio\Horizon Pad\` or `~/Library/Audio/Presets/Oberli Audio/Horizon Pad/`,
only if you also want your own presets gone.

## Quick start
Load it on a MIDI track; it opens on **Lagerfeuer** (Pad). **Browse** in the preset row lists every
preset by kind - Pad, In-between, Lead - and your own. Then: **ATTACK** sets how quickly notes
come in, **FILTER** how bright the whole pad is, **REVERB** how much room is around it.
It plays from C0 to C7; keys outside that range stay silent on purpose.
Parameters: [`docs/parameters.md`](docs/parameters.md). Presets: [`docs/presets.md`](docs/presets.md).
