# UI review, Tier P - 2026-10-17

Tier: **P** (Product), phase: **final** - one design, content and style set, the motion
pass (2026-10-07) and the performance pass (2026-10-14) landed. Reviewed with
ux-ui-design-bob 1.2.0: the eight lenses, `references/audio-plugins.md` (sections 2-14)
and the plugin release gate in `joint-plugin-workflow.md`. Evidence: offscreen renders
of the real editor from this commit with `HorizonPadGuiSnapshot` at 100%, 150% and
200% (default patch; playing a chord; FILTER 0% night and FILTER/REVERB/WIDTH 100%
golden hour; a preset recall with the title showing; the knob sheet), the code for
what a screenshot cannot show (keyboard, accessibility, resizing, host menus), and the
contrast numbers measured in `HorizonScene.h`. The HTML audit scripts do not apply to a
native JUCE editor; `contrast.py` was used for colour pairs.

**Goal and assumptions.** Users: worship keys players and producers, mostly in Ableton
Live, with a controller under their hands and the DAW on the same screen; the job is
"find a sound in the first minute, shape it with eight knobs, keep it". Context: dark
room, a 13-16" laptop or one studio screen, sessions of hours.

## What works well (keep this)

- **The layout is the signal flow.** PITCH/MOD, four pads, MACROS, OUTPUT, left to
  right, eight equal columns - a Launchkey's eight knobs. One main thing per card; the
  core controls (four VOL knobs) are the biggest things on screen and show their value
  permanently.
- **The one memorable element is the instrument's own metaphor**: the sky follows
  FILTER (night to golden hour), the ridge hazes with REVERB, the glow spreads with
  WIDTH, the sun rises on open. Motion only ever says "the sound changed"; it is not
  decoration (visual-design.md 6, audio-plugins.md 6).
- **Every control has a one-line tooltip** in the user's words; a recalled preset shows
  its name and one-line description under the wordmark for four seconds.
- **Consistent knobs**: same ring geometry, same drag model, same hover/drag warmth on
  all ten; text never below 12.5 px; pills 5.3:1, wordmark 3.6:1 (large text) at the
  design palette, and every palette keeps those at or above 4.7:1 and 3.35:1.
- **Cheap**: the editor paints at 30 fps only what moved, 3.7 ms for a full redraw at
  Retina (measured 2026-10-14), and nothing animates when hidden.

## Walkthrough (new user, the core job)

"Find a sound, make it darker and wider, keep it." Start: the sky rises, Lagerfeuer is
lit, the pads glow as you play - clear where you are. Browsing: click a pill, the title
tells you what it is; after six pills the row must be scrolled and nothing says so
(finding above). Shaping: FILTER and WIDTH are under "MACROS - shape the air"; the sky
darkening with FILTER confirms the change at once - this is the best moment in the
editor. "Keep it": "+ Save preset" is visible and the strip asks for a name; the new
pill grows into the row. No hesitation except the scroll, and one ambiguity: after
editing, is this still Lagerfeuer? (the "modified" finding).

Returning user: eight knobs, nothing hidden, A/B with a copy button - fast the 20th
time. Keyboard-only and screen-reader users: not served (finding above).

## Release gate checks the owner has to run (joint-plugin-workflow.md)

Ableton Live macOS is confirmed; still owed in Live on Windows and in Waveform: editor
opens without dialogs, space bar reaches the transport, scaling 100 / 150 / 200% (and
on Windows moving the window between screens of different scale), automation recorded
from the GUI plays back into the GUI, save/reopen keeps sound and preset name, open
and close the editor 20 times while playing without a CPU step, and the generic editor
from names and value strings alone (the "73 %" finding shows there).

## Deliberately unchanged

- **Dark only.** Tier P asks whether the plugin gets light, dark or both; the editor
  *is* a sunset scene that moves from night to golden hour, so the question is
  answered by the brief, not by a toggle. Contrast is held in every palette.
- **Macro knobs at 37 px** - judged by the owner in the plugin on 2026-10-06 and kept.
- **The 1080-wide eight-column layout** - it is the controller mapping; only the scale
  should become adjustable, not the arrangement.

2. Double-click reset, modified mark, preset arrows (2 each) - half a day together, all
